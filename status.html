Where we are.

Protocol, wallet, history, packaging and recovery components have been built and exercised privately. The work now is to bring the current source, four-coin configuration, public builds and participant operations together.

This is a dated development update, not live telemetry or a completion percentage.

02

The first Testnet
doesn’t need every future product.

An offered feature needs its own qualification. Keeping the first online release focused makes the boundaries understandable.

For supported online participation

  • Exact source, network configuration and participant release.
  • Current correctness, four-chain operation and fault/recovery checks.
  • Reproducible builds, release verification and supported installation.
  • Bootstrap, test-coin access, observation and incident/reset guidance.

Separate feature releases

  • Complete funded offline-cash and device/radio workflows.
  • Mobile-store releases, games and collectible products.
  • Live rewards, prizes, payment providers and redemption.
  • Hardware sales, hosting and paid support services.

03 / AFTER PUBLIC ENTRY

Testnet is a test.
Mainnet is another release.

Public participation is meant to produce evidence: real network behavior, defects, operator experience and recovery under stress. It does not turn test coins into money or certify the system for real funds.

Public entry requires the review and safety evidence applicable to that release. Independent professional audit, broader public-network evidence and separate mainnet testing remain outstanding. There is no fixed launch countdown.
