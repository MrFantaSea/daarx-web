01

The one to spend.

L10N takes the privacy work into spending. It has its own state and monetary rules while participating in the shared mining architecture. Offline cash is a separate, unfinished part of the work.

Shared work. Separate rules.

L10N has native state and transaction validity. Shared mining work does not turn it into a DAARX balance, wrapped token or automatic entitlement to another chain’s funds.

A payment cannot end at the tap.

Offline work must cover how value is loaded, exchanged, settled and recovered. Replay resistance, fraud limits and the behavior of real devices all matter. The complete public offline product is not available.

The unfinished part is not the whole story.

Protected custody, pending-transaction journals, native transaction paths and off-host recovery have private implementation evidence. Pouch-state recovery exists at its tested scope; that does not prove the entire offline payment cycle.
