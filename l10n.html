ECONOMICS

Why would people need L10N?

To pay someone. A spending coin gets demand from people who acquire it to use it, businesses that accept it, and payments that settle in it. L10N is designed for that role.

01

How it works.

Private digital cash.

L10N is designed for spending, with native balances and transaction rules. Its purpose is payment activity between people and businesses.

The offline-payment work.

The offline layer is being developed around the entire payment: loading, in-person exchange, later settlement and recovery.

The wallet remembers an interrupted payment.

Pending journals and protected custody retain the information needed to reconcile a transaction. Native transfer and recovery paths have been exercised in private builds.
