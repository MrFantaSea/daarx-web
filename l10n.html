01 / PURPOSE + PRINCIPLES

Money moves.
The details don’t.

L10N is money for circulation. The selected FEM model combines a permanent 1 L10N per paid block with a finite distribution bonus. This keeps a nominal miner incentive after the bonus ends; its purchasing power and actual security depend on participation, costs and demand.

01

THE PURPOSE

Money for passing on.

L10N separates everyday circulation from long-duration savings. It has its own balances and monetary rules, with private amounts and the family’s full-chain privacy work.

02

THE SECURITY BUDGET

Keep paying the people who secure it.

The selected FEM model adds a finite 77.7-year bonus to a permanent 1 L10N per paid block. The bonus rises toward year 7.77, then declines. Fees go to miners. Continuing issuance dilutes balances; a nominal reward floor cannot guarantee security. There is no premine, presale, founder reward or fee burn.

03

THE OFFLINE LAYER

Cash has to survive a lost connection.

The offline work covers loading, local exchange, receipts, later settlement and recovery. A separate anti-fraud bond backs defined obligations. It is committed capital, not an interest-bearing deposit.

02 / ISSUANCE + ALLOCATION

Security keeps getting paid.

All mined. No premine, presale or founder reward. Transaction fees stay with miners; the design does not burn them.
