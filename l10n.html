01 / PURPOSE + PRINCIPLES

Money moves.
The details don’t.

L10N is money for circulation. Its monetary design keeps a permanent positive mining reward, with fees also going to miners. A continuing security budget is part of the money itself.

01

THE PURPOSE

Money for passing on.

L10N separates everyday circulation from long-duration savings. It has its own balances and monetary rules, with private amounts and the family’s full-chain privacy work.

02

THE SECURITY BUDGET

Keep paying the people who secure it.

A permanent positive mining tail keeps a continuing reward inside the monetary design. Fees also go to miners and stay in circulation. The policy has no premine, presale, founder reward or fee burn.

03

THE OFFLINE LAYER

Cash has to survive a lost connection.

The offline work covers loading, local exchange, receipts, later settlement and recovery. A separate anti-fraud bond backs defined obligations. It is committed capital, not an interest-bearing deposit.

02 / ISSUANCE + ALLOCATION

Security keeps getting paid.

All mined. No premine, presale or founder reward. Transaction fees stay with miners; the design does not burn them.
