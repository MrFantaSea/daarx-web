WHY THIS MONEY EXISTS

Money moves.
The details don’t.

L10N is money for circulation. Its monetary design keeps a permanent positive mining reward, with fees also going to miners. A continuing security budget is part of the money itself.

ISSUANCE + ALLOCATION

The structure is a declining reward followed by a fixed, nonzero tail. The current reference curve uses 50 L10N per block, declining to 1. Those numeric parameters and the exact blocktime remain candidates while the economic model is reconciled.

All mined. No premine, presale or founder reward. Transaction fees stay with miners; the design does not burn them.
