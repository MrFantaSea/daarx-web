DAARX / Accountable authority

Accountable authority

OPEN

OPEN is for activity that should be accountable. People, institutions and agents can have public accounts, defined authority and a record of what they did.

OPEN

ECONOMICS

What gives OPEN a use?

Institutions and software agents need to pay, delegate and account for their actions. OPEN makes those relationships part of the native system. The demand case is transactions and activity that need that structure.

01

How it works.

People, institutions and agents.

OPEN distinguishes these actors and gives their authority an explicit scope. A mandate describes who can act, for whom and within which limits.

A record of the action.

Public transactions and receipts make obligations inspectable. Backing and restitution connect the action to what stands behind it.

Payments, delegation and agent work.

OPEN supplies the authority side of accountable execution. Institutions can delegate activity; agents can act under mandates; providers can return evidence of the work.
