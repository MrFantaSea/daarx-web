01

Authority you can inspect.

OPEN covers accountable authority: who may act, under which mandate, with what backing and what receipt. It is built for human, institutional and agent activity, rather than anonymous machine payments alone.

Who is allowed to do what?

OPEN makes actors, authority and mandates explicit. A human, an institution and an AI agent are not interchangeable identities. The system needs to make the scope of an action inspectable.

A claim needs a record.

Public transactions and receipts are part of the accountability model. Backing and restitution describe obligations that must be implemented and tested; they are not a promise of a live guarantee service.

Beyond a concept page.

Protected signing, an outbox, supported transfer paths, encrypted recovery and agent/mandate components exist in private work. The current public authority and activation package has not been released.
