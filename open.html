03 / ACCOUNTABLE AUTHORITY

Authority with limits.

The idea ↗ The monetary rules ↗

01 / PURPOSE + PRINCIPLES

Give permission.
Keep the limits.

OPEN gives delegated activity its own economic structure. Principals fund agents, mandates limit what they can spend, and HALO puts capital behind Agent value acts.

01

THE ACCOUNTS

Know who is allowed to act.

Human, Institution and Agent are distinct native account classes. Institutions use threshold authority. Agents act for a principal under mandates rather than inheriting unlimited control.

02

THE LIMITS

Permission has a budget.

Per-transaction, per-epoch and lifetime spending caps follow the mandate ancestry. Delegation cannot multiply that allowance. Fees consume it too; the maximum ancestry depth is seven.

03

THE BACKING

Put capital behind the action.

HALO backs admitted Agent value acts. Public accounts and committed records make the authority inspectable. The current objective-fault path concerns same-nonce equivocation; broader restitution and arbitration are separate work.

02 / ISSUANCE + ALLOCATION

A visible allocation.

The current subsidy sends 11% to the endowment and the remainder to miners. The designed dividend is disarmed; its share currently goes to miners.
