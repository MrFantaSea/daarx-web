Public accounts and bounded authority for people, institutions and agents.

WHY THIS MONEY EXISTS

Give permission.
Keep the limits.

OPEN gives delegated activity its own economic structure. Principals fund agents, mandates limit what they can spend, and HALO puts capital behind Agent value acts.

Per-transaction, per-epoch and lifetime caps apply through the mandate ancestry. Delegating a mandate does not multiply its budget.
