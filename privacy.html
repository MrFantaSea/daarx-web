Privacy notice

Know what leaves
your browser.

This notice covers the information site and its optional hardware-interest form. Reviewed November 4, 2026.

Browser ↓ GitHub Pages ↓ only if you submit interest Web3Forms → Proton → Labs

Operator

Desiderata Labs LLC operates this DAARX-branded information site and the hardware-interest form. DAARX is its registered alternate name. Protocol governance and commercial services are separate. No store, checkout, account or paid support service is offered on this site.

Browsing

The public pages use no advertising trackers, analytics scripts, accounts or marketing cookies. Fonts and visual effects are served with the site. The motion control changes only the current page; it does not store a preference. GitHub Pages may process ordinary network, device, delivery, security and server-log metadata.

What the form sends

Your email address; optional name or handle; selected hardware concept; access-model, membership, hosting, accessory and merchandise interests; optional notes; your privacy-notice acknowledgment; and delivery metadata. Do not enter secrets, payment-card data, identity documents, wallet keys or recovery phrases.

Purpose

To record and manage non-binding hardware interest, answer questions, prevent abuse and contact you if a relevant compliant offer or material update becomes available. A submission does not create an order, account, financing application or protocol allocation. We do not sell personal information or make automated eligibility decisions.

Providers

GitHub Pages hosts the site. Web3Forms processes and relays the form. Proton receives the message. These independent providers may process network, device, delivery, anti-abuse and log metadata under their own terms and notices. We disclose information to operate the site and form, respond to you, protect the service or comply with law.

Retention

An active interest record is kept only as long as needed to manage the pre-launch list and follow up, and no longer than 24 months after your last interaction unless a shorter legal or operational period applies. Provider copies, backups and logs follow provider retention and may not be immediately removable by us. Deletion from records we control is completed within 30 days unless preservation is legally required.

Your control

Submitting interest is optional, non-binding and free. Ask to access, correct or delete a record, or withdraw from follow-up, at daarxmoney@proton.me. We may ask you to verify control of the email address before changing or deleting a record.

Children and security

The form is not directed to children; minors should not submit it. Internet transmission and email cannot be guaranteed perfectly secure. Access is limited to the people and providers needed to operate the form and follow up. No payment data is requested.

Future changes

A future store, checkout, account, support or analytics feature needs an updated notice before its processing starts. This notice describes the current static site and hardware-interest relay.
