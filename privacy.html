Who operates the site

Desiderata Labs LLC operates the DAARX information site and hardware-interest form. DAARX is its registered alternate name. Protocol governance and commercial services are separate.

Browsing

Pages, fonts and visual effects are served with the site. There are no advertising trackers, analytics scripts, site accounts or marketing cookies. The motion control changes only the current page. GitHub Pages may process ordinary network, device, delivery, security and server-log metadata.

Your enquiry

The hardware form sends an email address; optional name; hardware, access-model, membership, accessory and merchandise choices; optional notes; privacy acknowledgment; and delivery metadata. Web3Forms relays the submission and Proton receives the message for Desiderata Labs LLC.

Purpose

We use enquiries to understand interest, answer questions, prevent abuse and follow up about a relevant offer or material update. We do not sell personal information or make automated eligibility decisions. A hardware enquiry records interest rather than creating an order or account.

Retention

An active interest record is kept only as long as needed, up to 24 months after your last interaction unless a shorter period applies. Provider logs, backups and copies follow provider retention. We delete records we control within 30 days of a verified request unless legal preservation is required.

Your choices

Enquiries are optional and free. Ask to access, correct or delete a record, or withdraw from follow-up, at daarxmoney@proton.me. We may verify control of the email address first.

Care with information

The form is for adults. Keep payment data, identity documents, wallet seeds and credentials out of the notes. Access is limited to the people and providers needed to operate the enquiry and follow up. Internet transmission and email have ordinary security limits.

Changes

This notice covers the current static site and enquiry relay. New account, commerce, support or analytics features require an updated notice before that processing begins.
