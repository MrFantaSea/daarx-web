Bonded assurance

Nexus / NXXXS

NXXXS is for paid work between systems: relaying state, proving a payment, carrying out an instruction or standing behind a service. Providers put native collateral behind their commitments.

NXXXS

ECONOMICS

Where does NXXXS demand come from?

Providers need NXXXS for operating balances and collateral. Customers use it to pay for services. Service providers earn those fees. Those uses give the native coin a direct role in the service economy.

01

How it works.

Work that someone pays for.

Relayers, proof providers, solvers and venues can charge for services in the design. Consumers pay when they use those services.

A provider commits NXXXS.

A service declares capacity and obligations, backed by native collateral. The protocol ties evidence and declared penalties to those commitments.

The assets stay native.

NXXXS coordinates evidence and service payments across independent systems. Ordinary DAARX, L10N and OPEN transactions retain their own native rules and fees.
