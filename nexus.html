WHY THIS MONEY EXISTS

Useful work.
Something behind it.

NXXXS gives service providers operating balances and collateral, and gives customers a way to pay for useful work. Relayers, proof providers, solvers and venues have distinct roles.

ISSUANCE + ALLOCATION

A ramp. A tail. Paid work.

7.77 target years to peak
0.1 candidate tail / block
2% finite founder share

The candidate ramps toward 10 NXXXS per block around year 7.77, then declines to a permanent 0.1 reward. Base fees and rent burn. Priority fees go to miners; service payments go to the chosen provider. Final public identity and parameters remain part of release preparation.

The reference candidate assigns 2% of subsidy to a finite founder stream during roughly seven target years. The stream ends before peak issuance. Miners receive the remaining subsidy.
