01 / PURPOSE + PRINCIPLES

Useful work.
Something behind it.

NXXXS gives service providers operating balances and collateral, and gives customers a way to pay for useful work. Relayers, proof providers, solvers and venues have distinct roles.

01

THE WORK

Pay for something that happened.

Relays, proofs, solvers, venues and settlement services are the demand paths. A customer pays a chosen provider for a supported service. An ordinary sibling-chain transfer does not incur a NXXXS toll.

02

THE COMMITMENT

The provider puts something up.

Providers maintain operating balances and commit native collateral to declared capacity and obligations. Evidence is checked under the selected rules. A service fee pays the provider; a bond remains committed capital until its rules release or penalize it.

03

THE ALLOCATION

A founder share with an end.

The reference schedule assigns 2% of gross subsidy to a finite founder stream for roughly seven target years. It ends before the candidate’s year-7.77 peak. Miners receive the rest; the stream is part of scheduled issuance.

02 / ISSUANCE + ALLOCATION

A ramp. A tail. Paid work.

The reference candidate assigns 2% of subsidy to a finite founder stream during roughly seven target years. The stream ends before peak issuance. Miners receive the remaining subsidy.
