Work that has to prove itself.

The fourth line joins verifiable work to declared commitments: objective evidence, bonded service execution, venue assurance and native-settlement routing. It has its own native unit and state.

Show the work.

The design verifies objective evidence against declared conditions. OPEN can authorize an actor and its mandate; this layer checks the evidence and declared bonded consequences of the work.

Independent systems can make commitments.

Service execution, venue assurance and native-settlement routing are part of the fourth line’s scope. Implementation and selected private integration exist. Complete four-chain public operation and production adapters remain to be qualified.

A fourth coin, not a toll on the other three.

Ordinary valid DAARX, L10N and OPEN transactions do not require NXXXS or owe it an automatic fee share. The coins remain native to their own rules and are not automatically wrapped into one asset.
