01 / PURPOSE + PRINCIPLES

Private money.
Long horizons.

DAARX is private capital with a fixed issuance path. New issuance rises toward year 7.77, then declines through a finite schedule. Most of the supply is still ahead at the peak.

01

THE DISTRIBUTION

Early doesn’t mean all of it.

Issuance rises toward year 7.77 instead of starting at its maximum. At that peak, about 22.19% of the literal ceiling is scheduled to have been issued. Most of the distribution is still ahead.

02

THE OWNER

A balance is personal.

Ordinary transfer amounts stay hidden. Full-chain membership proofs let the chain check a spend without identifying which earlier output it spends. One-time destination keys keep a reusable recipient address off the ledger.

03

THE ALLOCATION

Mining writes the first receipt.

Miners receive the entire subsidy and transaction fees. The retained monetary policy has no premine, presale or founder reward. The issuance schedule eventually reaches zero; fees then pay for mining.

02 / ISSUANCE + ALLOCATION

A later peak. A finite supply.

No premine. No presale. No founder reward. Miners receive the block subsidy and transaction fees.
