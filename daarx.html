ECONOMICS

Why hold DAARX?

The holder’s bet is demand for private capital. The supply follows a known schedule and eventually stops growing. People have a reason to hold it if they want those properties in money.

01

How it works.

The supply follows the code.

DAARX has an integer emission schedule that eventually reaches zero. The schedule defines how new coins enter circulation.

A spend can be checked without publishing its history.

Full-chain membership proofs target membership in the valid chain without identifying the earlier output being spent. Amount commitments keep the arithmetic verifiable while hiding the amount.

New coins come from mining.

DAARX uses RandomX parent work. The shared architecture also carries child commitments for L10N, OPEN and NXXXS.
