THE ECONOMICS

What would make
these coins valuable?

People using them.

DAARX gives people a reason to hold private capital. L10N is for money changing hands. OPEN handles activity that needs public accounts and defined authority. NXXXS lets people charge for services and put collateral behind the work.

Mining rewards and paid services are ways to earn coins in the design. The holder’s bet is that enough people will want to own or use them.

01

The four coins.

DAARX

Private savings.

Private savings. Fixed issuance. Eventually, zero new coins.

DAARX uses hidden amounts and full-chain membership proofs. The aim is to let someone prove a valid spend without publishing their balance or payment history.

About DAARX ↗

L10N

Private spending.

Private cash for payments between people and businesses.

L10N has its own balances, transaction rules and monetary parameters. It shares the family’s mining architecture and privacy work.

About L10N ↗

OPEN

Public accounts.

Public accounts and accountable activity for people, institutions and agents.

OPEN records who can act, the mandate they act under, and the obligations attached to the action. It includes backing, receipts and restitution in its design.

About OPEN ↗

Nexus / NXXXS

Proof and collateral.

Paid services, proofs and native collateral.

The protocol connects service requests, evidence and bonded commitments. Relayers, proof providers, solvers and venues have distinct roles in that system.

03

Where people fit.

MINING

Earn newly issued coins.

The block-reward rules define mining rewards. Shared work connects participation across the four-chain architecture.

SERVICES

Charge for useful work.

Relaying, proofs, settlement coordination and accountable execution are service roles in the design. Providers can charge fees and commit collateral.

OWNERSHIP

Choose what you want to hold.

The reasons differ by coin: private capital, spending balances, accountable activity or the operating needs of a service network.
