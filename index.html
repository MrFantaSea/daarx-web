01

Keep. Spend.
Authorize. Verify.

Shared architecture does not mean interchangeable money. Each coin has its own state, rules and reason to exist.

Private capital

The one to keep.

Private value for the long run. Deterministic issuance, hidden amounts and full-chain membership privacy.

DAARX is the private-capital side of the system. Its consensus work includes an integer emission schedule, amount commitments, full-chain membership proofs and a short public fee ladder.

Explore DAARX ↗

Private cash

The one to spend.

Private spending for everyday exchange. Shared mining work, its own monetary rules, and an offline-cash layer in development.

L10N takes the privacy work into spending. It has its own state and monetary rules while participating in the shared mining architecture. Offline cash is a separate, unfinished part of the work.

Explore L10N ↗

Accountable authority

Authority you can inspect.

Public accounts, mandates and receipts for people, institutions and agents. Authority should leave evidence.

OPEN covers accountable authority: who may act, under which mandate, with what backing and what receipt. It is built for human, institutional and agent activity, rather than anonymous machine payments alone.

Explore OPEN ↗

Bonded assurance

Work that has to prove itself.

Proof, coordination and bonded commitments. The fourth native coin supports work that can be checked.

The fourth line joins verifiable work to declared commitments: objective evidence, bonded service execution, venue assurance and native-settlement routing. It has its own native unit and state.

Explore Nexus / NXXXS ↗

Nexus / NXXXS is the fourth coin’s working identity. Final public naming is still open.

Privacy for value.
Evidence for authority and work.

DAARX and L10N target private value. OPEN makes authority, mandates and receipts inspectable. Nexus / NXXXS checks evidence and declared bonded commitments.

A valid payment on one chain does not become a payment on every chain. Ordinary DAARX, L10N and OPEN transfers do not require NXXXS or pay it an automatic share.

01 / WALLETS

A key needs a way home.

Private work includes wallet creation, protected signing, reviewed transfers, pending journals, encrypted backups and recovery. A supported public wallet is part of participant delivery.

02 / APPLICATIONS

There’s life around the coins.

Shared accounts, games, collectibles and Pride Pot components are in development. Game balances and rewards are separate from the four native coins. Public apps, funding and payment services still have work to finish.
