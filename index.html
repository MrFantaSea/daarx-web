01 / THE TRUST BOUNDARY

Privacy should be infrastructure.

A transparent base ledger can leave addresses, histories, and payment relationships open to analysis. DAARX and L10N target a different default: private value for people. OPEN makes authority public and bounded. NXXXS connects paid services to evidence and collateral.

RELATIONSHIPS REMAIN LEGIBLE

RELATIONSHIPS ARE THE PRIVATE LEDGER

MODEL / TRANSPARENT BASE LEDGER

The ledger remembers.

Public transaction histories can make relationships visible long after a payment. Pseudonyms alone are not the same thing as privacy.

MODEL / PRIVATE VALUE

The relationship is the secret.

DAARX and L10N use hidden amounts and full-chain membership proofs. The chain checks the spend without publishing the amount or naming the earlier output.

03 / THE PRINCIPLES

Who gets
the money.

The distribution matters as much as the supply. Each coin makes a different choice. You should be able to see it.

01

DAARX

Mined, from beginning to end.

No premine, presale or founder reward. Issuance rises into a delayed peak, then winds down to zero.

02

L10N

A security budget that continues.

All issuance and fees go to miners. A permanent positive tail keeps funding security. Fees stay in circulation.

03

OPEN

A public endowment allocation.

The current subsidy allocates 11% to the endowment and the rest to miners. The designed dividend is disarmed.

04

NXXXS

A finite founder stream.

The reference candidate assigns 2% of subsidy to the founder stream for roughly seven target years. It ends before peak issuance.
