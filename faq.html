What are the four coins?＋

DAARX is for private capital. L10N is for private spending. OPEN handles accountable authority and public receipts for people, institutions and agents. Nexus / NXXXS is the fourth line, for proof, coordination and bonded assurance. Its final public name and ticker are still unsettled.

How far along is the system?＋

Protocol, transaction, wallet, history, packaging and recovery components have substantial private implementation and testing evidence. Work is now focused on the exact public release, current four-chain qualification and the operations needed for public participation. That is more than a set of designs; it is not a released public network.

Can I join the public Testnet today?＋

Not yet. Public Testnet preparation is the current stage. The participation page will change when the supported release, network configuration, verification instructions and public endpoints are available.

Is there a launch date?＋

There is no committed public Testnet date on this site. The next release depends on current qualification and an explicit cutover decision. The old calendar target is not a substitute for that work.

Where are the wallet and miner downloads?＋

There is no supported public participant package to download here yet. Private wallet and mining work exists. The public release needs exact source and configuration, reproducible builds, release signatures, installation instructions and recovery guidance.

Does shared mining make this one coin?＋

No. The four coins have independent native state and rules. The architecture shares parent mining work with child-chain commitments; it does not merge balances, wrap the coins into a single asset or make a transfer on one chain a transfer on all four.

Does every transfer need Nexus / NXXXS?＋

No. Ordinary native DAARX, L10N and OPEN validity and fees do not depend on NXXXS. The fourth line adds explicit assurance and coordination functions; it is not an automatic toll on the other three.

Does OPEN only serve AI agents?＋

No. OPEN covers human, institutional and agent authority, mandates, backing, receipts and restitution. Its public accounts are meant to make authority and obligations inspectable.

Will the first Testnet include offline payments and mobile games?＋

Only features that have their own supported release and qualification. A valueless online Testnet can open without a finished offline-cash product, app-store release, hardware store, funded rewards or external payment providers. Those features have separate release work.

Is privacy guaranteed?＋

No. Hidden amounts and membership proofs target ledger privacy. Devices, network observation, software defects, operational mistakes and future cryptanalysis still matter. The integrated system has not completed an independent professional security audit.

Are coins for sale? Will Testnet coins have value?＋

No coins are sold on this site. Public Testnet coins will be for testing and have no monetary value. A test balance is not a claim on mainnet coins. Nothing here promises earnings, appreciation or a return.

Should I buy a miner now?＋

The site does not offer a supported public mining release or hardware sale. Hardware figures are unverified research estimates. An interest submission is not an order, financing application, reservation or priority claim.

Is a public bounty open?＋

No. A public program needs a published scope, reporting channel, safe-harbor terms, response expectations, award rules and confirmed funding. The security page describes the work ahead; it is not an invitation to attack systems.

What happens to my hardware-interest details?＋

Desiderata Labs LLC receives the information you choose to submit. Web3Forms relays it and Proton receives the message. The privacy notice covers the purpose, retention and how to request access, correction or deletion. No order or account is created.
